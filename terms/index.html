3. The 100 in 100 Triple Guarantee

The "100 in 100 Triple Guarantee" referenced in marketing materials is a real, contractual commitment between the Company and the Client, governed by the conditions in this Section 3 and by the more specific terms contained in the signed Engagement Agreement.

3.1 The Guarantee

Subject to the conditions set forth in this Section 3 and in the Engagement Agreement, if the Company fails to deliver one hundred (100) qualifying Sales Appointment Opportunities within the one-hundred (100) day window beginning on the official campaign launch date, the Client is entitled to all three of the following:

  1. $2,000 cash payment — paid in U.S. dollars to the Client.
  2. Full refund of management fees paid — refund applies to the Company's management fees only, and does not include advertising spend or third-party platform costs.
  3. Continued service at no additional management fee — until the one-hundredth Sales Appointment Opportunity is delivered, conditional on the Client's continued ad spend at the agreed level during the continuation period.

3.2 Sales Appointment Opportunity — Definition

A "Sales Appointment Opportunity" means a homeowner who:

  • submits qualified contact information through the funnel installed by the Company,
  • meets the Client's pre-defined Ideal Customer Profile criteria as set out in the Engagement Agreement (typically including, but not limited to: project category, budget range, geographic eligibility, and decision-maker status),
  • books a calendar slot for a sales conversation with the Client, and
  • is exclusively routed to the Client (not sold or routed to any other contractor).

Inbound leads who fail to qualify against any of these conditions, or who book and then no-show without rescheduling, do not count as Sales Appointment Opportunities for the purposes of the guarantee.

3.3 Start of the 100-Day Window

The 100-day window begins on the official campaign launch date as defined in the Engagement Agreement. The campaign launch date is the date on which the Company turns on paid advertising in the Client's accounts. The campaign launch date is conditional on the Client first completing all required setup steps, including but not limited to: providing the Company with read/write access to required ad accounts, providing approved photo and video assets, attending the kickoff "War Room" call, and authorizing the agreed initial advertising spend.

3.4 Cooperative Obligations

The guarantee is conditional on the Client's good-faith cooperation with the Company throughout the 100-day window. Specifically, the Client agrees to:

  • maintain the agreed minimum monthly advertising spend, in the agreed accounts, for the entire 100-day period (and during any continuation period under Section 3.1);
  • provide and maintain the access required for the Company to install, operate, and optimize the system, including but not limited to access to Meta Ads Manager, the Client's CRM, and any related platforms;
  • respond to inbound Sales Appointment Opportunities within the response-time window agreed in the Engagement Agreement;
  • refrain from making unilateral changes to active campaigns, ad creative, ad copy, or audience targeting without the Company's prior written consent;
  • attend all required onboarding and ongoing strategy or "War Room" calls scheduled by the Company; and
  • provide approved photo, video, and copy assets within the timelines agreed in the Engagement Agreement.

The guarantee in this Section 3 is voided, at the Company's reasonable discretion, if the Client:

  • fails to meet any cooperative obligation in Section 3.4;
  • unilaterally pauses, modifies, or cancels active campaigns without the Company's prior written consent;

  • refuses recommended optimizations from the Company that, in the Company's reasonable professional judgment, are necessary to deliver on the guarantee;

Any guarantee claim must be made by the Client in writing to the Company within fourteen (14) days following the close of the 100-day window.
