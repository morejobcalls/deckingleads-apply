Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-28 · Operated by SeasonProof Growth LLC

This is a draft. This Privacy Policy is a starting framework summarizing how SeasonProof Growth LLC collects, uses, and protects information submitted through the Site. It should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before being relied upon as a legal instrument, particularly for compliance with state-level privacy laws (CCPA, CPRA, VCDPA, etc.) and the federal TCPA.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how SeasonProof Growth LLC ("Company," "we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information you ("you," "Client") submit through the website at apply.deckingleads.com (the "Site") or through any related communication channel (phone, text, email, web form).

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you interact with the Site:

  • Information you submit voluntarily. When you complete an application form on the Site, you submit information including: your name, email address, phone number, business revenue range, geographic market, business stage, and selected pain points or business descriptors.
  • Automatically collected technical information. When you visit the Site, our hosting provider and analytics tools may log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring URL, pages visited, and approximate timing of visits.
  • Advertising and conversion data. If you arrive on the Site from a paid advertising source (such as Meta), we and our advertising partners may receive standard click-tracking data, conversion-event data, and cookies that enable advertising attribution and audience optimization.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use information you submit to:

  • determine whether your business meets our Ideal Customer Profile criteria for further outreach,
  • contact you to schedule a strategy call (by phone, text, email, or mail),
  • provide the services you have engaged us to provide if and when you become a Client,
  • improve the Site, our marketing, and the system delivered to Clients,
  • comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers. We share information with third-party service providers who help us operate the Site and our business, including but not limited to: hosting providers, CRM platforms (such as GoHighLevel), email and SMS delivery services, scheduling platforms, and analytics and advertising platforms (such as Meta).
  • Legal compliance. We may disclose information if required by applicable law, court order, or government request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of the Company or others.
  • Business transitions. If the Company is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

5. Cookies and Tracking

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site, remember user preferences, measure ad performance, and improve the Site over time. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may limit some Site functionality.

6. Communications Consent and Opt-Out

By submitting an application form, you consent to receive communications from the Company by phone, text, email, or mail using automated technology, even if your number is registered on a state or national Do Not Call list. Standard message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by:

  • replying STOP to any text message you receive from us,
  • using the unsubscribe link in any email we send,
  • contacting us directly at the email address in Section 10.

7. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of any information transmitted to or stored by us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to:

  • request access to the personal information we hold about you,
  • request correction of inaccurate information,
  • request deletion of your information,
  • opt out of certain uses of your information for advertising or analytics.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address in Section 10. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site and the Company's services are intended for business owners and operators. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us at the email address in Section 10 and we will delete it.
