A Few Things Worth Knowing

Most builders ask these first.

I've been burned by agencies. Why would this be any different?

That's exactly why this offer exists. We start with an Agency Audit on every agency you've hired before us. It's a written document showing why they failed and what we're doing differently. Then we tie our own neck to a clear number: 100 exclusive opportunities in 100 days, or you get $2,000 cash, a full refund, and we keep working free until you hit it. You don't have to trust my words. You can trust the structure.

This still sounds too good to be true.

That's the right reaction, and I'd be skeptical too. It is too good, on paper. The reason it works is the system has the math and the proof behind it, and we'd rather pay than apologize. Miss the 100? You get $2,000 cash, every dollar of your management fee refunded, and we keep working free until you hit it. Anyone can write a guarantee. They can't copy actual calendars, pipelines, and recordings from contractors who said the same thing on day one. Justin. Brian. Eric. All of them changed their mind because the system kept delivering.

What if the appointments are junk?

We don't sell shared, recycled leads. These are exclusive, pre-qualified appointment opportunities. The funnel filters for project type, budget, timing, and decision-makers before they hit your calendar. Your CRM gets scripts and automations so your team stops chasing ghosts.

I don't want to get locked into another contract or lose my accounts.

You own the accounts, the data, and the system from day one. The sprint is 100 days. If we stop working together after that, nothing breaks. The engine stays in your business.

I'm not sure we can handle more volume.

Let's be honest. If you truly can't run 3–5 appointments per day, this will overwhelm you. In that case, we pause and fix ops first. This offer is for companies ready to step on the gas, not add more chaos.

You're not even in my state. How do you know my market?

Fair question, and one every out-of-state client has asked before hiring us. We're not local and we don't pretend to be. What we are is operators who've installed this exact system in deck businesses across the country. The market intelligence comes from data, not zip codes — your best customer profile, your competitor pricing, your top-converting project types, and the call recordings that tell us what your buyers actually say.
